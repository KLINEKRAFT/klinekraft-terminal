us-states.geojson — the built-in fallback basemap used when no offline street
map region is installed.

Geometry: U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary files (public domain), as
packaged in us-atlas 3.0.1 (states-10m.json) by Michael Bostock. Converted from
TopoJSON to GeoJSON, rounded to 3 decimal places, Hawaii and the territories
removed to match the offline street map coverage (lower 48 + Alaska).
